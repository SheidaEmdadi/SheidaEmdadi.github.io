I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science from McMaster University, where I worked on Communicating Statecharts (CSC), a visual notation designed to make concurrency approachable even for novice programmers. Alongside the notation I built CSCDraw, a model-driven development tool, and designed a pilot study comparing CSC to traditional ways of teaching concurrency. This work was supervised by [Dr. Christopher Anand](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/faculty/dr-christopher-anand/) and [Dr. Spencer Smith](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/faculty/dr-spencer-smith/), and presented at ACM ITiCSE.

Since June 2025 I've been an R&D Computer Scientist with the Fondation STaBL Foundation, researching and building educational tools in computer science — a continuation of a relationship with the group that began as a volunteer research intern back in 2022.

The thread running through this work — finding representations that make hard-to-reason-about systems legible — is what's drawing me toward a Ph.D. I'm looking to extend it toward human-computer interaction, human-AI interaction, and trustworthy AI, including applications in healthcare, and I'm currently reaching out to prospective supervisors whose work touches these areas.

I'm always glad to hear from researchers working on making complex systems easier to understand and trust — feel free to get in touch.
