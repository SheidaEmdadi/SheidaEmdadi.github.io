Re-implemented an energy-based anomaly detection method for graphs using a GCN encoder in PyTorch Geometric — building the full pipeline for edge perturbation, sub-graph likelihood-based energy scoring, and training. Benchmarked against nine state-of-the-art baselines (DOMINANT, GAAN, CONAD, and others) across four real-world datasets (Weibo, Reddit, Disney, Books), matching state-of-the-art performance among the reproduced baselines and landing within roughly 2–9 ROC-AUC points of the original paper's results.

Course project for Machine Learning on Graphs, Winter 2024 (Instructor: Dr. Lingyang Chu).
