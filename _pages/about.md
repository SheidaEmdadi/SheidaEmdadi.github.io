I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science from McMaster University, where I worked on [Communicating Statecharts (CSC)](https://macsphere.mcmaster.ca/items/e532c886-d36e-46e5-a754-c1ed71f19fa4), a visual notation designed to make concurrency approachable even for novice programmers. Alongside the notation I built [CSCDraw](https://stablfoundation.org/assets/images/CSCDraw.html), a model-driven development tool, and designed a pilot study comparing CSC to traditional ways of teaching concurrency. This work was supervised by [Dr. Christopher Anand](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/faculty/dr-christopher-anand/) and [Dr. Spencer Smith](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/faculty/dr-spencer-smith/), and presented at ACM ITiCSE 2025.

Since June 2025 I've been an R&D Computer Scientist with the STaBL Foundation, researching and building educational tools in computer science, a continuation of a relationship with the group that began as a volunteer research intern back in 2022.

My passion for academia is drawing me toward a Ph.D. I'm currently looking for a Ph.D. position where I can extend my background and skills toward Artificial Intelligence (AI), human-computer interaction, human-AI interaction, reinforcement learning, and trustworthy AI, including applications in healthcare, or CS education.
