The purpose of using *Pivot or Not* stage in the Design Thinking process is
to allow the design team to decide whether they should continue with their current design,
or they should pivot to a new design idea.

In this regard, every team member should answer a set of questions,
and finally, the whole team should decide whether to pivot or not.
The discussion in this stage is very important.

For simplifying teaching, we used a Highway analogy for this stage.
