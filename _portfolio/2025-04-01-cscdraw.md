CSCDraw is a visual Model-Driven Development (MDD) tool built to support Communicating Statecharts (CSC), a visual notation designed to make concurrency approachable even for novice programmers. It was developed as part of my M.Sc. [thesis](https://macsphere.mcmaster.ca/items/e532c886-d36e-46e5-a754-c1ed71f19fa4) at McMaster University, supervised by [Dr. Christopher Anand](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/faculty/dr-christopher-anand/) and [Dr. Spencer Smith](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/faculty/dr-spencer-smith/), and is connected to the CSC work presented at ACM ITiCSE.

**[Click the title above to open the live tool](/files/cscdraw/)** — it runs entirely in your browser, no installation required.
